Receivables and cash-flow monitor

See who owes what, who to chase first, whether collections are slowing down and what cash to expect over the next eight weeks.

The problem

The business is profitable on paper but cash is often tight. The aged receivables report is exported once a month, the sales team does not see which customers are late, and nobody can say with confidence how much cash will come in over the next few weeks.

The owner wants to know who to chase first, whether collections are getting slower, and what the bank balance will look like after payroll and supplier payments.

The data

Built on a realistic dataset modelled on a UAE distributor. Three tables: customers with credit terms and limits, every invoice and credit note with its payment date, and the bank account movements.

Approach

  1. Receivables as at any date. An invoice is open on a date if it was issued on or before that date and not yet paid. This gives the AR balance for every month end, not only today.
  2. Ageing. Open amounts are grouped by days past the due date: not yet due, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and over 90 days.
  3. DSO trend. Days sales outstanding = open receivables divided by the last 90 days of credit sales, times 90, at each month end. It is shown against the average credit terms (about 50 days).
  4. Collections priority. Score = overdue amount, weighted up for the age of the oldest overdue invoice, for exceeding the credit limit and for a slow payment history over the last 12 months. Each customer gets a suggested action.
  5. Cash-flow view. Bank movements are grouped by category per month, with the closing balance. Expected collections for the next 8 weeks use each customer's own average lateness.

The rules are written in Python here (analyse_receivables.py); in a client build they become Power BI measures, with Power Automate sending the alerts.

How it would work for a real client

Tools

  • Power BI for ageing, DSO and the cash view
  • Power Automate for daily overdue alerts per salesperson and a weekly collections list in Teams
  • SharePoint or OneDrive for exports of invoices, receipts and the bank statement
  • Optional: Power Apps for logging collection calls and promised payment dates

What you provide

  • Exports of open and paid invoices, credit notes and customer receipts
  • Customer master with credit terms, limits and salesperson
  • Bank statement exports (CSV) and a list of recurring payments such as payroll and rent
  • Microsoft 365 with Power BI Pro licences
  • Agreement on the alert rules and who receives them

Indicative effort: about 2 to 3 weeks of build after the diagnostic, depending on how payments are recorded and how many bank accounts are involved. The scope and a fixed price are confirmed after the paid reporting diagnostic. Nothing on this page is a client result.
